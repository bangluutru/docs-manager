PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT

Japanese Business Document & Sales Management App

Version: 1.0
Product type: Internal web application
Primary market context: Japanese business operations
Primary language: Japanese
Deployment: Cloudflare
Status: Planning baseline

⸻

1. PRODUCT VISION

Build a lightweight internal web application for creating, issuing, storing and managing standardized Japanese business documents while providing simple sales, invoice and payment tracking.

The application should replace fragmented workflows currently performed with Excel, Word, manually edited PDF templates and separate document folders.

The product must be:

* simple;
* fast;
* visually professional;
* consistent;
* suitable for Japanese business practice;
* reliable for daily company operations;
* optimized for standardized document creation;
* capable of basic sales/payment visibility.

The application is intentionally NOT:

* a CRM;
* an ERP;
* an accounting system;
* an inventory system;
* a procurement platform;
* an HR system;
* a project-management system.

The core philosophy is:

Enter business information once, reuse it safely, generate professional Japanese business documents consistently, and retain reliable document history.

⸻

2. PRIMARY PRODUCT OBJECTIVES

The application must enable employees to:

1. Create standardized Japanese business documents quickly.
2. Produce aesthetically professional A4 documents.
3. Reuse counterparty, product and company information.
4. Convert one business document into another without re-entering data.
5. Generate reliable PDF and print output.
6. Maintain issued-document history.
7. Track invoices and payments.
8. View basic sales information.
9. Search historical documents efficiently.
10. Standardize Japanese business-document practices across the company.

Target performance with master data already available:

* Create a normal 見積書 in under 60 seconds.
* Convert 見積書 → 請求書 in under 10 seconds.
* Locate an existing document in under 10 seconds.

⸻

3. TARGET USERS

Primary users are internal company employees involved in:

* sales administration;
* general administration;
* purchasing;
* billing;
* payment tracking;
* business operations.

Typical users should not need accounting expertise.

The system must reduce the need for employees to understand formatting rules or manually reproduce Japanese business conventions.

⸻

4. PRODUCT SCOPE

V1 supports six primary business documents.

Sales-side documents

1. 見積書 — Quotation
2. 納品書 — Delivery Note
3. 請求書 — Invoice
4. 領収書 — Receipt

Purchase-side documents

5. 発注書 / 注文書 — Purchase Order
6. 注文請書 — Order Confirmation

These documents share one common document engine and design system.

Do NOT build six unrelated document applications.

* 得意先 — customer;

22. TAX ENGINE

Tax calculation must be a dedicated business-logic layer.

Support:

* 10% standard tax;
* 8% reduced tax;
* 非課税;
* 不課税;
* 免税.

Support configurable rounding:

* 切り捨て;
* 四捨五入;
* 切り上げ.

Support:

* 税込;
* 税抜.

Tax behavior must be deterministic.

Do NOT let document UI components independently calculate taxes.

All tax calculations must be covered by automated tests.

Support mixed tax rates within one document.

25. DOCUMENT IMMUTABILITY

Once a document is ISSUED, it must not be silently modified.

27. DOCUMENT CONVERSION

47. SNAPSHOT PRINCIPLE

Critical historical information must be snapshot into the document.

Example:

If ABC株式会社 changes its address next year, an invoice issued this year must continue showing the original address.

Therefore do not render historical issued documents solely from current master data.

Relevant master data must be captured as document snapshots.

This applies especially to:

* recipient;
* issuer;
* bank details;
* registration number;
* line-item descriptions;
* prices;
* tax information.
